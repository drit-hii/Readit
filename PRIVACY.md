# Readit – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Readit is a reading tracker. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## Data stored on your device
Your books, reading sessions, goals, quotes, voice-note transcripts and bookmarks are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us or to any server we control. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Camera
The camera is used only to read the barcode (ISBN) on the back of a book. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.

## Microphone and speech recognition
The microphone is used only while you record a voice note. Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, on device when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple may process the audio under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Audio recordings are not kept. Note summaries are generated on device.

## Book lookup
When you scan a book, only its ISBN number is sent to Google Books and, if needed, Open Library to find the title, author and page count. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

## Widgets and Live Activities
Your favourite quotes and the running timer are shared with Readit's widgets on your device only.
